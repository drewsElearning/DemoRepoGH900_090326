01

Frame the change

A good pull request makes the why, what, and risk legible. Keep the diff focused and give reviewers the context they need.

02

Review as a team

Review is a shared quality loop, not a gatekeeping ritual. Ask precise questions and explain the reasoning behind suggestions.

03

Merge with intent

Choose a merge strategy that preserves the story you want the repository history to tell, then close the loop with confidence.
